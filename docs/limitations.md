# Limitations

- Synthetic results do not establish investment alpha or real-world model skill.
- LLM replays over historical dates are retrospective: model pretraining may contain later
  knowledge even when prompts contain only dated evidence.
- Daily next-close execution is an approximation, not a trade simulator.
- Synthetic prices omit many real corporate-action, liquidity, tax, and delisting details.
- The optimizer is sensitive to expected-return and covariance estimation error.
- A three-year interval is too short to establish broad robustness.
- Provider outputs and prices may change outside recorded mode.
- The application does not place orders or provide individualized financial advice.

Negative, flat, and inconclusive outcomes are expected and must remain visible.
